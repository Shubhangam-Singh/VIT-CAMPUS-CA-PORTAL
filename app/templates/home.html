VIT Campus CA Portal

A small Public Key Infrastructure for the VIT Vellore campus. Students and faculty request X.509 certificates, the Registration Authority approves them, and the Issuing CA signs them.

How the trust chain works

  1. Root CA Kept offline. Self-signed. It signs only the Issuing CA certificate.
  2. Issuing CA Online. Signs end-entity certificates and publishes the revocation list (CRL).
  3. Registration Authority (RA) Checks who the applicant is and approves or rejects the request. It signs nothing itself.
  4. End-entity certificates Student login, faculty e-mail, server TLS and code signing certificates.
