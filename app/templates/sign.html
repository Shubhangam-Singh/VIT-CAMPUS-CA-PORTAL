{{ icon('file') }} What you get

A small JSON file to keep next to the signed file:

  • sha256fingerprint of the file's exact bytes
  • signatureRSA-PSS over that hash, made with your private key
  • signer_certificate + chainso anyone can check it leads to the VIT Root CA
  • signed_atthe time of signing (your claim; see the verify page)
