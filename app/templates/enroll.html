A. Upload a CSR recommended

You make the key pair yourself; only the request is sent. Your private key never leaves your computer.

How do I make a CSR?

With OpenSSL (Git Bash):

openssl req -new -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout my.key -out my.csr -subj "/CN=me"

Or, for this demo, run python scripts/make_sample_csrs.py and pick a file from demo_work/.
